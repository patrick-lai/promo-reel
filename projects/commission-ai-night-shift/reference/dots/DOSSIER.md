## 9. The ONE thing that makes it work

A warm, believable conversation: people are relieved and delighted by an agent that reports small finished things and asks only for the decisions that matter, and the product is shown as a big readable screen inside that human moment.

## 10. What is transferable to this product

- The same arc: a person hands off work and gets relief; the agent reports in short human sentences and 'checks in when something needs your approval' (commission-ai's Needs you / Allow once is literally that).
- Screens shown big, legible, 3-6 s, inside a human moment (we have real recordings of the chat, plan board, Needs-you card, reviewer approval, PR merged).
- Title blooming out of darkness; warm tungsten night interiors; eyeline-motivated cuts; reaction close-ups; ending on a pull-back to the whole 'workshop' (we have the real Workshop town view at dusk and dawn).
- Two-voice dialogue: agent reports, human answers casually.

## 11. What is NOT transferable and why

- Live-action actors on a physical set, shot on cinema cameras: we have no crew; decision needed on generating people scenes (conflicts with AGENTS.md rule 1, real footage only).
- Spoken dialogue between a human and the agent as a product feature (dots is a voice/chat assistant): commission-ai has no voice mode; the voices would be dramatised narration of what the UI shows (conflicts with rule 3, no invented claims; needs a decision on what the voices may say).
- The mascot characters and the ChatGPT brand screens: not our IP and not our product; use the commission-ai UI and the Workshop robots.
- Non-work life tasks (wedding planning, kids' lessons, recital): commission-ai is a coding-agent product; use only work that the footage shows (tickets, PRs, reviews).
- A 148 s runtime: a promo for this product should be about 60 s.
